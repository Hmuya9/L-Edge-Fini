# Le Fade - Sweet Spot Analysis & Customer Mix Optimization

## **Sweet Spot: 60% Standard + 40% Deluxe**

### **Why This Mix Works:**
- **Balanced profitability**: $9.99 Standard + $37.50 Deluxe margins
- **Optimal barber utilization**: 8-10 customers per barber
- **High total profit**: $300+ per barber per month
- **Brand appeal**: Large, engaged customer base

## **Customer Distribution Targets**

### **Per Barber Sweet Spot:**
- **6-8 Standard customers** (break-even + profit)
- **4-6 Deluxe customers** (high margin)
- **Total**: 10-14 customers per barber

### **Monthly Profit Per Barber:**
- **Standard**: 8 × $9.99 = $79.92
- **Deluxe**: 6 × $37.50 = $225
- **Total**: $305 profit per barber

## **Growth Phase Targets**

## **Never Want to Be Here:**

### **❌ Too Many Standard (80%+ Standard)**
- **Problem**: Low profit per customer ($9.99)
- **Result**: Need 20+ customers per barber to be profitable
- **Risk**: Barber burnout, quality decline
- **Example**: 80 Standard + 20 Deluxe = $1,200 profit (vs $1,800 optimal)

### **❌ Too Many Deluxe (70%+ Deluxe)**
- **Problem**: High barber costs ($22.50 per customer)
- **Result**: Limited market size, price sensitivity
- **Risk**: Smaller addressable market
- **Example**: 30 Standard + 70 Deluxe = $1,500 profit (vs $1,800 optimal)

### **❌ Too Few Customers Per Barber (<6)**
- **Problem**: Below break-even point
- **Result**: Losing money on every barber
- **Risk**: Unsustainable business model

## **Red Flags to Monitor:**

### **🚨 Customer Mix Red Flags**
- **>80% Standard**: Low profitability
- **>70% Deluxe**: Market size limitations
- **<6 customers per barber**: Below break-even

### **🚨 Operational Red Flags**
- **>15 customers per barber**: Quality decline
- **<4 customers per barber**: Underutilization
- **High churn rate**: >10% monthly
