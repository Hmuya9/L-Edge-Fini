# Le Fade MVP - Simple & Focused

## What We Built
A minimal haircut subscription platform with just the essentials:

### Core Pages
1. **Landing Page** (`/`) - Hero section + plan overview
2. **Plans Page** (`/plans`) - Standard ($39.99) vs Deluxe ($60) with WhatsApp signup
3. **Booking Page** (`/booking`) - Simple calendar to book appointments
4. **Barber Dashboard** (`/barber`) - View and manage appointments

### What's NOT Included (Yet)
- ❌ Stripe payments (using WhatsApp for now)
- ❌ Database (using mock data)
- ❌ User authentication (simple forms)
- ❌ Email notifications
- ❌ Complex admin features

## Why This Approach
- **Fast to build** - Get customers in 1-2 days
- **Easy to test** - Simple flows, easy to debug
- **Low cost** - No external services yet
- **Focus on core** - Just what you need for first 8-10 customers

## Sweet Spot Strategy
- **Target**: 60% Standard + 40% Deluxe customers
- **Profit per customer**: Standard $9.99, Deluxe $37.50
- **Break-even**: 6th Standard customer, 2nd Deluxe customer
- **Goal**: 8-10 customers per barber

## Next Steps (When Ready)
1. Add Stripe for real payments
2. Add simple database (SQLite or PostgreSQL)
3. Add email confirmations
4. Deploy to Vercel

✅ **MVP Complete** - Ready for your first customers!
